# Frequently asked questions

## Why was a distribution refused
The common reasons are: the card is not assigned to this shop, the card is not eligible, the quantity is more than the remaining entitlement for the month, or the shop does not have enough stock. The error message states which rule failed.

## How do I see how much ration I have left
Beneficiaries can open My Ration to see the entitlement, the quantity already received, and the quantity remaining for the current month. Dealers can enter a card number on the Distribute Grain page to check the same figures.

## My login does not show my ration card
After registering, a beneficiary account must be linked to a ration card by a Government Officer. The officer enters your username when adding you as a family member on the card.

## How do I change my password
Open the menu under your name and choose My account, then enter your current and new password. A new password needs at least 8 characters.

## How do I report damaged stock
Warehouse managers use Report damage on the Warehouse Stock page. Dealers use Report damaged stock on the Shop Stock page. The quantity is deducted through a ledger entry and cannot exceed the stock held.

## Where can I download reports
Government Officers, Super Admins and Auditors can open Reports, choose monthly, district-wise, shop performance or complaints, and download the CSV file.
